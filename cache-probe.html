cache probe

As-you-type generation re-runs the whole network for a one-character change. This measures how much actually changes, which bounds how much could be reused.

Stage 1 is free (tokenizer only): how many of the 77 token slots move when you add one character. Stage 2 downloads the ~650 MB text encoder and measures how far that propagates into the [1,77,1024] conditioning tensor the U-Net consumes.
